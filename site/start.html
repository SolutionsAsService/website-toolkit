Your first part, step by step

You need a web page you can edit as HTML: a file on your computer, or the HTML view of your site builder. Nothing to install.

  1. Pick a part

    Open Parts and choose one, for example the Contact form. Each part page shows it live at phone, tablet and laptop width.

  2. Copy it

    Press Copy code. You get one block: a <style> part that makes it look right, and the HTML that draws it. Keep them together.

  3. Paste it into your page

    Paste the block inside your page's <body>, where you want the part to appear. The <body> is the part of the page people see. It already works: every colour and size has a built-in default.

  4. Put in your own words

    Change the text and links. The places meant for you are marked data-slot in the code, and the part page lists what each one is for. Leave the labels and the words in aria-… and alt: they are what screen readers say.

  5. Give it your colours (optional)

    Copy the block from tokens.css into your page's <head>, the hidden top part of the page, inside a <style>. These 15 lines are the tokens: one colour, size or font each. Change one and every part follows. The sandbox lets you try colours and copy the result.

Then

Stack several parts into a page in the sandbox, or start from a whole template: a garden studio in Fremantle, an event in Lagos, an illustrator's portfolio in Spanish, a product launch.

Some parts need one more thing, said on their page under Needs. The forms need an address to send to (a form service or your own server); the map needs to reach openstreetmap.org.
