Paste it and it works

No framework, no build, no account. Each part carries its own scoped styles, and every colour and size has a fallback, so a part works on its own and takes on your theme when tokens.css is present.

See it bare

X-ray strips a part's styles and labels every element and every data-slot, the places you or an agent fill in. The sandbox shows the exact document you copy: what you see is what you paste.

Checked, with the results published

Each part is rendered alone at 375, 768, 1280 px in every theme and checked for overflow, labels, target size, focus and contrast. 18 of 18 pass; each part page says what was checked.
