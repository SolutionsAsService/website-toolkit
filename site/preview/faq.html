Questions

What does a small garden cost?

Most front gardens under 40 square metres cost between $3,500 and $6,000 planted, including two seasons of care.

Will I need to water it?

For the first summer, yes, about once a week. After that most of our plantings live on rain alone.

When is the best time to plant?

Autumn and early spring. We plan all year and plant in those two windows.
