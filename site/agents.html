For agents

Everything on this site is also a file an agent can fetch. One JSON registry lists every part with its slots, needs, access notes, hash and check result.

Endpoints

/registry.jsonEvery part and template: id, code, summary, slots, needs, a11y, raw path, sha256, check result.
/llms.txtThe same in short Markdown, with the steps below.
/raw/tokens.cssThe token block. Optional: every part has fallbacks.
/raw/themes.json4 token presets.
/raw/parts/<id>.htmlA part: JSON header, scoped style, markup.
/preview/<id>.htmlThe part alone as a full page: what the check renders.

Compose a page

  1. Read registry.json; pick parts by group and summary.
  2. For each, fetch raw, verify sha256, and drop the <!--sr:part … --> header.
  3. Put the tokens' :root block in the head. To theme, override its variables from themes.json.
  4. Fill each data-slot element with the user's content. The part's slots object says what each one is.
  5. Keep labels, aria-* and alt. If you change a heading level, keep the page's order (one h1).
  6. Meet the part's needs, for example a form endpoint.

Example

curl -s https://website-toolkit.shadw.app/registry.json | jq -r '.parts[] | select(.group=="Forms") | .raw'
curl -s https://website-toolkit.shadw.app/raw/parts/contact-form.html

What the check means

The check last ran on 2026-11-11. It rendered each part alone at 375, 768, 1280 px in the 4 themes and probed: no sideways scroll; every form field has a label; every stat has a source line; no target smaller than 24 px with another target within 24 px (links inside running text exempt); after a real Tab press, every focused element shows an outline of 2 px or more at 3:1 contrast against what lies just outside each of its sides, or a two-tone ring whose tones differ by 3:1; text contrast of 4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text, against the nearest opaque background. 18 of 18 parts passed. A part's check.pass in the registry is that result. It is not a guarantee about your page once you change the part.
