F1 Or divider

A rule with a word in the middle, for separating two alternatives, such as two ways to sign in.

Passes the check, rendered alone at 375, 768, 1280 px in each of the 4 themes: no sideways scroll; every form field has a label; every stat has a source line; no target smaller than 24 px with another target within 24 px (links inside running text exempt); after a real Tab press, every focused element shows an outline of 2 px or more at 3:1 contrast against the surface behind it; text contrast of 4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text, against the nearest opaque background.

Open in sandbox

Previewed with tokens.css and a three-line reset (box-sizing, body margin, image width), the same head the sandbox writes. The code below is the part alone.

Code

The part as one block. It works pasted alone; add tokens.css to theme it.

<style>
.sr-or{display:flex;align-items:center;gap:14px;max-width:28rem;margin:24px auto;padding:0 var(--sr-space,16px);font-family:var(--sr-font-body,system-ui,sans-serif);color:var(--sr-muted,#4f4b6a)}
.sr-or::before,.sr-or::after{content:"";flex:1;border-top:1px solid var(--sr-line,#d6cdf2)}
.sr-or span{font-size:.8rem;font-weight:700;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:.08em}
</style>
<div class="sr-or" role="separator" aria-label="or"><span aria-hidden="true" data-slot="word">or</span></div>
