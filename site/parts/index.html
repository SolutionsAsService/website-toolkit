Parts

18 parts in 7 groups. Each page has a live preview at three widths, X-ray, the code to copy and what the part needs.

A Navigation

  • A1Navigation barBrand name plus links. Below 640 px the links fold into a menu that opens without JavaScript (details/summary).
  • A2BreadcrumbShows where a page sits in the site. The last item is the current page and is not a link.

B Heroes

  • B1Split heroHeadline, one sentence and two actions beside an image. Stacks text-first on narrow screens.
  • B2Statement heroOne very large sentence and a single action, no image. For a page whose claim is the design.

C Content

  • C1Feature gridA heading and three to six short features, each a title and one or two sentences. Columns reflow from three to one.
  • C2Numbered stepsHow it works in three to five numbered steps. The numbers come from an ordered list, so reordering renumbers them.
  • C3Stat rowThree or four large numbers, each with a label and its source. A number without a source line is not allowed by the check.
  • C4TestimonialOne quotation, large, with the person's name and role. Use real words from a real person, with their permission.
  • C5Pricing tableTwo or three plans side by side, each with a price, what it includes and one action. One plan can be marked as the usual choice.
  • C6Questions and answersQuestions that open to show their answers, with no JavaScript. Each one can be linked to by its id.
  • C7Image galleryA grid of images with captions. The first image can span two columns. No lightbox and no JavaScript.
  • C8Call-to-action bandA full-width band with one sentence and one action, to close a page.

D Forms

  • D1Contact formName, email, an optional subject and a message, each with a visible label. Posts to any endpoint you set; there is no backend in this repo.
  • D2Email signupOne email field and a button in a band, with a line saying how often you will write.

E Places

  • E1Map and addressAn OpenStreetMap embed (no API key) beside the address written as text, with a link to open the map. The text works even when the map does not load.

F Dividers

  • F1Or dividerA rule with a word in the middle, for separating two alternatives, such as two ways to sign in.
  • F2Section markA short centered ornament between sections of long text: three spaced marks instead of a full rule.

G Footers

  • G1Footer with social linksName, a short line, page links, social links with simple drawn icons and text names, and the year. Icons are fixed at 20 px by width/height attributes, so they stay small even without the stylesheet.
