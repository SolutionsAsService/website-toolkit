# R2 · Critic: Provocateur (Shklovsky)

**Anchor used:** *Art as Technique* (Shklovsky): habit devours things. A device that stays fixed while everything around it changes becomes the habit.

**Observed:** index-{signal,riso,cobalt,mint}-desk.png, index-signal-phone.png. The buttons now change the whole ground (ultramarine, magenta, navy, green), with the header and band. The sprue frame, cards hung from spines like kit parts, is the strongest device and is not buried; on the phone it fills the first screen. Two things never change: the acid-yellow frame and the seven spine hues (A coral through G white). Signal parts sit on #f4f0ff (evidence), which reads as near-white in the screenshot. Riso parts sit on cream.

**R1 requests:** F3 (frame and ground) answered. F4 (Signal ground) half-answered. F2 (rank the hues) not answered: G is white, but the other six are still a legend.

**Findings:**
- F1 [claim] Each theme click now changes the whole object, so it is seen anew.
- F2 [defect] The rainbow spines are the same in all four colourways, so the most habitual device is the one constant.
- F3 [defect] Signal's pale lilac and Riso's cream read as paper again.
- F4 [defect] The numbered list on the left (1–5, one yellow pill) is the stock SaaS onboarding row.
- F5 [premise] The sprue makes "snap off" literal. It deserves the colour.

**Verdict:** OBJECT

**Requested change:**
- F1: none.
- F2: let the spines follow the theme. Give one group the frame yellow and set the rest a step off the ground.
- F3: make Signal's lilac visible, and give Riso's parts a colour that is not cream.
- F4: none (noted; usability owns it).
- F5: none.
